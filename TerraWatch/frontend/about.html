About TerraWatch

Satellite-informed ground-motion screening for a more resilient Addis Ababa.

THE PROJECT
⌖

Turning geospatial data into a clearer picture

TerraWatch is a prototype monitoring interface that brings backend radar displacement estimates, spatial model outputs, exposed-structure data, and point-level analysis together in one place. It is designed to help people explore where further investigation may be useful—not to replace field surveys, engineering judgement, or official hazard assessments.

Built for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026.
